About

Behavioral economist, teacher and science communicator.

I am a Mexican economist living in southern Chile. I studied economics at Universidad Veracruzana and applied macroeconomics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, and earned my MS and PhD at the University of Florida.

Since 2011 I have been on the faculty of the Instituto de Gestión e Industria at Universidad Austral de Chile in Puerto Montt. Before that I taught at Universidad Diego Portales, Universidad de Concepción and the University of Florida, and I have been a visiting professor in MBA programs in Peru, Paraguay and Mexico.

Write to me at carlos.pitta@uach.cl.
