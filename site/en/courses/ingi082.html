Introduction to Economics

The basic building blocks of the economic system, how it works in broad terms, and the tools economics uses to deal with scarcity.

Units

  1. Introduction to economic analysis

    The ten principles of economics; thinking like an economist.

  2. Microeconomic analysis

    Supply and demand, elasticity, public policy, market efficiency and externalities.

  3. Macroeconomic analysis

    National income, the cost of living, production and growth, unemployment, aggregate demand and supply, and monetary and fiscal policy.

Main textbook

Mankiw, Principles of Economics, plus lecture notes.
