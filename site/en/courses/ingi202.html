Econometrics I

The fundamentals of regression analysis, its assumptions, the problems that arise and how to fix them, in order to estimate and forecast multivariate economic phenomena.

Units

  1. What econometrics is about

    Causality, correlation and the conditional expectation E(Y|X).

  2. Bivariate regression

    Least squares estimation and inference.

  3. Multiple regression

    Estimation with several regressors.

  4. Hypothesis tests and goodness of fit

    Individual and joint inference.

  5. When classical assumptions fail

    Multicollinearity, heteroskedasticity, autocorrelation and specification.

  6. Special topics

    Dummy variables, nonlinear models and instrumental variables.

Main textbook

Gujarati and Porter, Basic Econometrics; Wooldridge, Introductory Econometrics; lecture notes.
