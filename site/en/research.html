Research

Behavioral and experimental economics, with happiness as the common thread.

The questions I work on

  • Happiness and well-being

    What makes people happy and how to measure it: experiments in India and Mexico, dictator games and happiness data.

  • Behavioral macroeconomics

    How people's expectations and biases add up across the economy.

  • Migration

    Earnings gaps between migrants and natives, using Mexican migration data.

  • Crime and fear

    How much victimization raises fear of crime, using a six-wave Chilean panel.
